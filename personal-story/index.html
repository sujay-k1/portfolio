how it all makes sense now?arrow_forward

Honestly, I spent way too much time on this portfolio. Not a cool thing to say in an industry that moves fast and ships faster. I also fear judgement about the ROI, that the outcome, even the quality, may not justify the time spent.

This is not the first time. Back in school, I used to spend considerably more time on school projects, trying to make them special, trying to make them look the best, adding quirks to them. My focus should have been on the book, on practicing problems. Perhaps, there was a reason, most others chose to get done with it. When I was introduced to Java, I spent multiple nights in front of my computer trying to make a game of chess with my very limited knowledge of algorithms. I got obsessed with the problem. I used co-ordinate geometry, which I was learning at school, to guide the movement of the chess pieces. There were multiple set backs, but seeing something in action that I had made gave me real joy! I finally completed it. There was no ROI.

I fell in love with cinema on the other hand. What a powerful medium! It could make me cry, fill me with joy, compassion, and curiosity. I wanted to become a filmmaker. I wanted to be able to build something that connected with people's lives on a deeper level. Made some movies in college (here's one I am really proud of), never made it again. I still dream of making cinema.

Again, no real ROI. I guess I did all of this for myself, to feel the power of being a creator. In making this portfolio, I think I reconnected with the part of me that has always loved building with passion.

Where does this all take me? Today, to being a product designer who gets obsessed with the problem. In a way, I am living my dream: building real things that connect with people's lives. This is how it all makes sense to me now!

I still second-guess my solutions. I still feel embarrassed when I look at work from the past. I still do not feel very confident about the design work I have produced. And here I am, finding work to pour all my creative urges into. Hit me up!
